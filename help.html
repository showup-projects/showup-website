Getting Started guide

A new account gets a short guide on the Home tab, Getting Started: a few steps that fit the way you came into Showup. Each step has a button that opens the screen where it's done, and it's ticked off by itself once you've done it, wherever in the app you do it. The guide is there for an account's first 30 days, for accounts created on or after October 21, 2026.

Your steps

If you joined a Space someone else runs — as a Member, Coach or Staff — your guide is Start today's workout:

  1. Join a space — already done when the guide appears.
  2. See today's workout and log it — log a score on a workout.
  3. Log your first PR — add a personal record.
  4. Book a class — only if you can book: the Space has booking on and you hold a membership plan.

If you run a Space — you created it, so you're its Admin — your guide is Start your space, whatever kind of Space it is:

  1. Create a space — already done when the guide appears.
  2. Post your first workout
  3. Log your first score
  4. Create a class schedule — only if booking is on in a Space you run.

Three optional steps follow: Bring in members (with Open QR or Invite by Email), Write your first community post and Log your first PR. They're numbered on after the others and marked Optional step. They don't count toward "1 of 3 steps done", and you can finish the guide without them — so it works if you train on your own, too.

Running any Space puts you on the second guide, even if you've joined other Spaces as well.

How it works

  • The step to do now sits on a card with its button. Some buttons open a screen that highlights what to tap there — tap it, or tap OK, got it.
  • Steps can be done in any order. Once the required steps are done, the first optional step left takes the card.
  • When the required steps are done, the guide says "Now log every day and build your Showup", and Close takes it off Home.

Close it, and find it again

  • Close sits at the top of the guide. Before the required steps are done, it takes the guide off Home, with Undo for a few seconds.
  • The guide stays in the Account tab as the top row, Getting Started Guide: the same steps, each one tap from its screen, and — if you closed it before the required steps were done — Show on Home Again.
  • That row stays while any step is left, optional ones included. Once the required steps are done, it says how many optional steps are left.
  • The guide and the row go away once every step is done, or when the account's 30 days are up.

No Space yet? Every account without a Space, new or not, sees How would you like to start? on Home, with Join a space (the QR scanner) and Create a new space (see Create a Space).

What is a Space?

A Space is your home for tracking workouts — solo, with friends, or with a team you coach. It's where your sessions, workouts, scores, and leaderboards live. How many Spaces you can create depends on your plan, and each Space has its own members, sessions, and history.

When you create a Space, you pick one of four purposes:

  • Just for me — a private Space for your own training log.
  • Small group — workouts with a handful of friends or training partners.
  • Gym or Box — programming and leaderboards for a club, box, or team.
  • Training program — a structured program you publish for athletes to follow.

Create a Space

From the Home tab, tap the + at the end of the My Spaces header and choose Create a new space — or, if you don't have a Space yet, tap the Create a new space tile on Home. The flow walks you through up to six steps:

  1. Purpose — choose one of the four types (Just for me, Small group, Gym or Box, Training program).
  2. Name — give your Space a name (up to 30 characters).
  3. Sessions — pick at least one of the suggested session types or add your own (e.g., "Cardio", "Strength", "Monday WOD"). You can add more later.
  4. Booking — choose whether members can book classes in this Space. You can turn it on later in Space Settings. If you choose Just for me, this step is skipped.
  5. Membership plans — add the plans you'll assign to members, or tap Do it later. If you choose Just for me, this step is skipped.
  6. Customize — pick a color and an icon, or upload a logo image.
Required: name, purpose, and at least one session. Everything else — booking, membership plans, logo, color — is optional and can be changed later.

Update a Space

From the Home tab, open the ⋯ menu on the Space's card and choose Space Settings. You can change:

  • Name, type, and time zone
  • Booking on or off, and the membership plans you assign to members
  • Member privacy — hide the member list from members
  • Sessions — add new ones, rename or reorder existing ones, or archive ones you no longer use
  • Space Exercises — the Space's own exercises for % of PR

Logo, color, banner, and About

How a Space looks and what it says about itself are edited on its info page instead: open the same ⋯ menu and choose Space Info.

  • Banner, logo, and color — tap the photo icon at the top of the page, then Banner Photo or Logo & Color.
  • About — tap Edit next to it (Add if it's empty). Up to 2,000 characters.
  • Details — tap Edit (or Add) to set your website, Instagram, address, and city.

Space Settings and editing Space Info both require an Admin or Staff role. Members and Coaches can open Space Info but can't change it — ask an Admin or Staff member of your Space if you need a change. While a Space is archived, nobody can edit it until an Admin unarchives it.

Heads-up: if your Space has more active sessions than your current plan allows, session editing is frozen until you archive a few. Archiving is reversible, so this isn't permanent.

Space Exercises

A workout can give a load as a % of PR — "80% of your Back Squat 1RM" — and every member sees the weight from their own PR. Showup comes with 20 lifts for this, the Showup Exercises, in every Space. Space Exercises are a Space's own lifts on top of those: the ones Showup doesn't have, like Box Squat or Pause Bench.

Admins and Staff manage them. Open the ⋯ menu on the Space's card, choose Space Settings, and under Exercises tap Space Exercises. Changes there are saved right away. Coaches don't see this list, but they can pick its exercises when they write a workout.

Add an exercise

  1. Tap Add Exercise (or + at the top).
  2. Enter a Name (up to 30 characters) and choose a Category — one of the 12 that personal records use.
  3. Tap Save.
  • A Space Exercise is always scored by Weight, Higher is Better: a percentage only works on a lifted weight.
  • A name and category that match a Showup Exercise are refused, and so is a name the Space already uses.
  • A Space can have up to 500 Space Exercises.
  • While writing a workout, Admins and Staff can also add one straight from the exercise search: when nothing matches, tap Add "…" to Space Exercises.

Rename an exercise

Tap the exercise, change its name or category, and tap Save. The same screen shows how many workouts use it. Those workouts follow the new name, but PRs members logged under the old name don't count toward the new one — Showup warns you before you save.

Delete an exercise

Swipe its row left and tap Delete, or open it and tap Delete Exercise at the bottom. Showup first checks how many workouts use it and tells you. In those workouts it then shows as Removed exercise, and members no longer see a weight for it. Deleting can't be undone, but it never touches anyone's PRs.

Note: collaborative workouts can use Showup Exercises only.

Leave a Space

From the Home tab, open the ⋯ menu on the Space's card and choose Leave Space. You'll see a confirmation: once you leave, you'll need to be invited again to rejoin.

One-admin rule: the Admin of a Space is whoever created it, and the role can't be handed to anyone else. So if you're the Admin, you can't leave directly — archive the Space from Space Settings first, then leave. The app says the same thing if you try.

Archive a Space

Archiving retires a Space without permanently deleting it. Everything stays recoverable — you can unarchive at any time. There are two options when you archive:

  • Archive (keep media) — text, workouts, scores, and photos are all preserved in read-only mode.
  • Archive & remove media — deletes photos, videos, and media library content but keeps text content, scores, and metadata. Useful if you want to keep the workout history but free up storage.

Archived Spaces show an "Archived" badge in your membership list and are hidden from active views. They can be unarchived at any time.

Who can archive a Space?

Archiving or deleting a Space is an Admin-only action. See Roles & permissions for what every role can do.

Roles & permissions

Every member of a Space has a role that determines what they can do. Roles are cumulative — each role includes all the permissions of the roles below it, plus its own.

RoleCan
AdminEverything Staff can, plus:
  • Archive or delete the Space
  • Delete session types
StaffEverything a Coach can, plus:
  • Edit Space settings and session types
  • Manage members and assign roles (up to Staff)
  • Accept or decline join requests
  • Turn QR join requests on or off
  • Edit or delete any workout
  • Delete any score or comment
  • Moderate posts and media
  • Add, edit and delete Space Exercises
  • Turn booking on and manage membership plans
  • Delete schedules, and cancel the bookings on them
  • Create, edit or remove any member's membership hold in the current membership period
  • Renew a membership: add a period, grant bonus days, or set an exact expiry
CoachEverything a Member can, plus:
  • Create and edit their own workouts
  • Invite new members
  • Show and share the Space's QR code
  • See the member list even when the Space hides it from members
  • Add and manage their own media in the library
  • Create and edit schedules, be assigned to them, and add or remove participants
MemberThe default role:
  • Log scores on workouts
  • Add comments and reactions
  • Write community posts
  • View the community, members (unless the Space hides its member list) and Media Library
  • Book classes, join waitlists, and manage their own holds

Admins have full control of a Space. Staff can manage members and assign roles up to Staff (they can't create or remove Admins). Coaches focus on programming and media, and can run schedules. Members participate by logging scores, commenting, posting, and booking classes — it's the role new members join with, and the one most people keep.

Spaces: related concepts

Sessions

Every Space is organized by sessions (e.g., "Monday Strength", "Cardio Club"). Session names can be up to 20 characters. The free plan allows 2 active sessions per Space; paid tiers allow 5 to 10 depending on the tier.

Free plan limits: 2 active sessions and 5 members per Space, 2 Spaces of your own, and 1 collaboration. Paid tiers raise each of these — the app tells you which limit you've hit and what upgrading would give you.

Roles

Members can be Admins, Staff, Coaches, or Members. Only Admins can archive or delete the Space; Staff can edit settings and moderate. See Roles & permissions for the full breakdown.

Membership status

Active (full access), Invited / Pending / Requested (awaiting approval), Expired (past its end date — access has ended, but you're still on the Space's member list and an admin can bring you back by adding a period), or Archived (you left or were removed).

What is a Workout?

A Workout is a single structured unit of training — the movements, the scoring rules, and any coaching notes — that members can log scores against. (It's not the same as a session: a session is a recurring category inside a Space, like "Monday Strength", and it holds many workouts over time.) A workout belongs to a Space and a session, but it can also be shared with other Spaces through collaborations, creating a combined leaderboard across all participating members.

Create a Workout

Inside a Space, open the session you want and tap + to create a new workout. Creating and editing workouts requires a Coach role or higher. You'll fill in:

Required

  • Scoring — how members log the workout, picked from five tiles. Nothing is chosen for you (see Scoring below).
  • Content — the workout description (movements, sets, structure). Up to 1,000 characters. Type @ to add a weight worked out from each member's own PR — see % of PR.
  • Date — when the workout is programmed for.
  • Session — which session in the Space this belongs to.

Optional

  • Title (up to 30 characters)
  • Coaching notes (up to 2,000 characters)
  • A time for members to first see it (Schedule workout — see below)
  • Media references (up to 10 reference images, videos, or YouTube links, depending on your plan) — uploaded directly, or reused from your Media Library (see below)
  • Collaborating Spaces (invite other Spaces — see Collaborative workouts)

Scoring

The Scoring section sits right under the title, above Content. Nothing is chosen for you: until you tap one of its five tiles, Save stays off and the section says "Scoring must be selected". Pick the tile that matches what members will log:

  • For Time — members log their time. Fastest ranks first.
  • AMRAP — members log rounds + reps, or just reps. Most ranks first.
  • Strength — members log the weight of each set. Heaviest ranks first.
  • No Score — members only mark it done. There's no ranking.
  • Custom — you set the Score Type (weight, time, reps, or distance) and the Better Score (higher or lower is better) yourself. Save stays off until you've chosen a score type.

Depending on the tile, the same card shows a few more settings:

  • Has Time Cap — for For Time, and for a Custom time where lower is better. While it's on, members who hit the cap log rounds + reps instead of a time.
  • Rx/Scaled Options — the only setting a No Score workout has, so it sits right in the card.
  • Options — on every tile except No Score. It lists what's switched on and opens Scoring Options.

The tile you pick fills in sensible settings — For Time takes one score per member, Strength allows several — and Scoring Options is where you adjust them:

  • Rx/Scaled Options — members choose Rx or Scaled when they log a score. It's on unless you turn it off.
  • Allow Multiple Scores — members can log up to 15 scores, for example one per set. Final Score sets what ranks on the leaderboard: the smallest value (Minimum), the largest (Maximum), or all of them added up (Sum).
  • Reps per Round — for reps workouts, and for time workouts with a time cap. Set it for exact rep counting at official events; leave it blank for casual tracking, where members enter rounds + extra reps.
  • Tiebreaks — up to 8 extra values members enter with their score, checked in order (top first) when scores are tied. Without tiebreaks, tied scores are put in alphabetical order.
Note: scoring can't be changed once the workout is created — only its final score and tiebreaks can (see Edit a Workout). Check it before you save.

Schedule workout

Members see a new workout as soon as you save it. To post it ahead of time and show it to them later, turn on Set a time under Schedule workout, then choose the Visible from date and time.

  • When you turn it on, the time is set to 00:00 tomorrow — ready for tomorrow's workout at midnight — and you can choose any time up to a year ahead. A time that has already passed can't be saved.
  • The date and time are read in the Space's time zone, not your phone's. While Set a time is on, the section names the zone.
  • Until then, members don't see the workout at all. Admins, staff and coaches already see it — with a dashed outline and the time it appears for members — and can use it as usual: comment, log scores, edit or delete it.
  • At that time, it becomes an ordinary workout for everyone, and members get the usual new-workout notification.

Duplicating a workout doesn't copy its time: the copy is visible to members as soon as you save it, unless you set a time again.

About the Media Library

Your Space has a Media Library — a place to save reusable references so you don't re-upload the same media for every workout. Each item is one photo, video, or YouTube link with a title (up to 100 characters) and an optional note (up to 500 characters). Save a movement demo once, then attach it to as many workouts as you like. Coaches and above can add and manage media in the library.

Note: attaching media is plan-dependent (free plans can attach 1 per workout), and a workout that uses Media Library items can't also have collaborating Spaces — choose one approach per workout.

Edit a Workout

Open the workout and tap the edit icon. Within a Space, a Coach can edit their own workouts and Staff can edit any workout. For collaborative workouts, only the Space that created the workout can change its settings — Spaces that joined as collaborators can still log scores and comment.

What can't be changed

The Scoring card is locked once the workout exists, because changing it would conflict with scores members may have already submitted. It shows the workout's tile with the others faded, and the note "Scoring can't be changed after a workout is created." That covers:

  • The tile itself (For Time, AMRAP, Strength, No Score, or Custom) — and on a Custom workout, its Score Type and Better Score.
  • Has Time Cap.
  • Rx/Scaled Options, Allow Multiple Scores, and Reps per Round.

Under Options you can still change the Final Score of a workout with multiple scores, and its Tiebreaks. The content, title, date, session, coaching notes, and media references stay editable too.

Schedule workout

The Schedule workout section appears only on a workout that was saved with a time — a time can't be added later. Until that time, you can change it, or turn Set a time off and save to show the workout to members right away. Once members can see the workout, the time is fixed, and the section says "This workout is now visible to members, so the time can no longer be changed."

% of PR

Write a load as a percentage of a personal record — "Back Squat @80% of 1RM" — and every member sees the weight worked out from their own PR, in their own unit: "Back Squat @100kg" for a member whose 1RM is 125 kg, and the weight in pounds for one who uses lb. Anyone who writes workouts (Coach and up) can use it in Content.

Add a reference

  1. In Content, type @ at the start of a line or after a space — or tap % of PR on the bar above the keyboard.
  2. Percentage — type a number from 1 to 100, or tap a suggestion.
  3. Exercise — type to search, and pick one. The Showup Exercises built into Showup come first, then your Space's own Space Exercises.
  4. Rep Max — 1RM is picked for you; you can choose up to 20RM.

At each step, Return takes the highlighted choice. The reference becomes one block in the text, like Back Squat | @80% | of 1RM. You can also start with the exercise: type @squat, pick it, and the percentage starts at 100%.

  • Tap a part of the block to change it (Change Percentage, Change Exercise, Change Rep Max), or touch and hold it for Remove Reference. Backspace selects the block first and removes it on the second press.
  • References work in Content only, not in Coaching Notes.
  • A reference uses more of Content's 1,000 characters than it shows. When one won't fit, the bar says "Not enough room".
  • Exercise not there? Admins and Staff can add it on the spot with Add "…" to Space Exercises. Coaches see "Ask an admin or staff member to add it."

What members see

  • The weight from their best PR for that exercise and rep max, in their weight unit, with up to two decimals — on the workout and on its card in the Space.
  • A heavier lift at more reps counts too: if their 3RM is heavier than their best 1RM, a 1RM reference uses it, the same way their PR history counts rep maxes.
  • Without a PR for it, they see the percentage in a dashed outline, and the workout shows See your own weights with a button for each missing PR. Tap one, log the PR, and the weight fills in.
  • Show % switches the workout to percentages, and Show Weights switches back. Your device remembers the choice.
  • Admins, Staff and Coaches also see "Each member sees weights worked out from their own PRs."

Collaborative workouts

A collaborative workout can use Showup Exercises only. While Content uses a Space Exercise, the Collaboration section shows Collaboration unavailable — replace it with a Showup exercise to add collaborators. If collaborators are already picked, Save stays off until no Space Exercise is left.

If an exercise is renamed or deleted

Workouts follow a renamed Space Exercise. A deleted one shows as Removed exercise, keeping its percentage and rep max but no weight. When you edit the workout, tap Removed exercise to pick another exercise. Save stays off for a newly added reference to an exercise this Space doesn't have — one pasted from another Space's workout, for example.

Archive or delete a Workout

Showup automatically picks the right action when you remove a workout:

  • Archive (soft) — if anyone has logged a score, or if multiple Spaces are collaborating, the workout is archived rather than deleted. It's hidden from lists but score history and the leaderboard are preserved. It can be unarchived any time.
  • Delete (permanent) — if no scores have been logged and only one Space is involved, you can delete it outright. This can't be undone, so you'll see a confirmation prompt.

Editing or deleting any workout requires Staff; Coaches can remove their own workouts.

Collaborative workouts

A collaborative workout is shared by more than one Space. When you invite another Space, all of their members can log scores on the same workout, and a single combined leaderboard ranks everyone together — perfect for friendly competitions between gyms, partner programs, or training groups across cities. In the app this lives under the Collaboration section.

How it works:

  1. While creating or editing a workout, tap Add Collaborator (shown as "콜라보 스페이스 추가" in Korean) and pick the Spaces you want to invite.
  2. Each invited Space gets a pending invitation. An admin of that Space can accept (choosing which of their sessions the workout should appear in) or reject it.
  3. Once accepted, the workout appears in the invitee's Space, and their members can start logging scores on the shared leaderboard.
Note: the number of Spaces you can invite depends on your plan (free plans allow 1). A collaborative workout can't also use Media Library items — pick one approach per workout. Its % of PR references can use Showup Exercises only.

If a collaborating Space leaves

A Space that joined as a collaborator can leave the workout from its detail view. The workout stays active for everyone else, and that Space's members' scores remain on the leaderboard.

If everyone leaves

If every collaborating Space leaves and only the creator remains, the workout simply reverts to a normal single-Space workout for the creator — nothing is lost.

If the creator removes it

The creator can't "leave" their own collaborative workout, but if the creator deletes it while other Spaces are still collaborating, it's archived rather than permanently deleted. For the other collaborating Spaces it becomes read-only: they can still open the workout and view its full leaderboard and score history, but no one can add new scores, edit it, comment, or react (the score button shows "Scoring Disabled"). Only the original creator Space can unarchive it to make it active again.

Workouts: related actions

Log a score

Open a workout and tap Add My Score (Mark as Done on a No Score workout). Enter your result and any notes, then tap Save.

On a workout with multiple scores, you can log up to 15 — for example, one per set. Type each score and tap Add. You can also add it straight from the keyboard — with Add above the number pad on iPhone and iPad, or the ✓ key on Android. The workout's final score decides what ranks (see Create a Workout). If you tap Save while a score is typed in but not added, Showup asks first ("Score Not Added"): Add and Save, Save Without It, or Cancel.

Leaderboard

Every workout has a leaderboard that ranks all submitted scores using the ranking method and any tiebreaks the workout creator set. For collaborative workouts, the leaderboard spans every participating Space. A No Score workout's leaderboard lists everyone who marked it done, without ranks.

Comment & react

Leave comments on a workout and react to other members' scores to cheer them on.

What is Booking?

Booking lets a Space run scheduled classes that members reserve a spot in — with capacity limits, membership credits, and a waitlist. It's available on every plan, but you'll only see it in Spaces where an admin has turned it on.

Where to find it

  1. Open the Booking tab.
  2. Pick a Space, then swipe through the weeks to find a day.
  3. Tap a class to see its details, who's coaching, and how many spots are left.

Schedules and sessions are different things

A session is a recurring category inside a Space, like "Monday Strength" — it groups workouts over time. A schedule is a specific class at a specific time that people book into, and it can optionally be linked to a session. Each day of a repeating schedule counts separately, so capacity is tracked per day rather than across the whole series.

Don't see a Space in the Booking tab? Booking is a per-Space setting. If a Space you belong to is missing, its admins haven't enabled it.

Book a class

Once a Space has booking turned on, you reserve a spot straight from the calendar.

How to book

  1. Open the Booking tab and pick the Space.
  2. Find the day and tap the class you want.
  3. Tap Book. Your spot is confirmed straight away.

What affects whether you can book

  • Booking window — a class can open for booking a set number of days before it starts. Until then you'll see "Booking opens" with the date, and no Book button.
  • Capacity — limited classes show how many spots are left. When a class is full you can join the waitlist instead.
  • Credits — if the class requires a credit, booking spends one from your membership. Your remaining count is shown on the class's Membership card.
  • Your role — admins, staff and coaches book for free and never spend credits. The booking window still applies to them.

If a class fills up while you're looking at it, tapping Book puts you on the waitlist automatically and tells you your position — you don't need to tap again.

Cancel a booking

Open the class from the calendar or from My Bookings, then tap Cancel Booking. Whether your credit comes back depends on the cancellation policy that applies to that booking.

Cancellation policies

PolicyDo you get the credit back?
Always freeYes — whenever you cancel.
Free up to a deadlineYes, if you cancel at least the set number of hours before the class starts. Cancel after that and the credit is forfeited. The app tells you the deadline before you confirm.
Never freeNo — the credit is spent once the booking is made.

Worth knowing

  • The policy that applies is the one stored on your booking when you made it. If an admin edits the membership plan afterwards, bookings you already hold keep their original terms.
  • Once the class has started you can no longer cancel it.
  • Cancelling frees your spot, so the person who has waited longest on the waitlist is moved into a confirmed booking automatically.

Waitlist

When a class with limited capacity is full, you can join its waitlist and be moved in automatically if a spot opens up.

How credits work on the waitlist

  • A credit is taken when you join the waitlist, not when you get a spot. That reserves it so the same credit can't be spent on another class.
  • Nothing extra is charged if you're moved into a confirmed booking — the credit was already taken.
  • Leaving the waitlist always returns the credit, no matter how close to the class it is. You never held a spot, so no cancellation policy applies.

Getting a spot

  • The order is strictly first come, first served. There's no way to move up the queue.
  • When someone cancels, the person who has waited longest is moved in automatically and notified. You don't have to accept anything.
  • If the class starts and a spot never opened, your hold is released and the credit is refunded automatically.

The full waitlist is only visible to admins, staff and coaches. As a member you can see that you're on it, but not who else is.

Your membership and credits

A membership is your join record in a Space — your role, your status, the plan you've been assigned, and your credits. Open it from Account → My Memberships, or inside a Space from Community ▸ Members ▸ My Membership.

Credit types

TypeHow it works
Per weekA set number of credits each week, reset at the start of the week.
Per monthA set number of credits each calendar month, reset on the 1st.
Fixed packA total number of credits with no weekly or monthly reset. The pack starts over when an admin renews your membership — see When your membership is renewed below.
UnlimitedNo credit is ever spent, however many classes you book.

How your remaining credits are counted

  • Credits left = your allotment minus what you've used in the current period. Waitlist holds count as used until they're refunded.
  • Periods are worked out in the Space's time zone, not your phone's — so a Sunday-night class can land in a different week than you'd expect if you're travelling.
  • Your membership can have a start and an end date. Classes that fall before your start date can't be booked. Credit history lists every credit spent and returned.

When your membership is renewed

An admin renews you by adding a period of your plan — a month, a year, however long one term of the plan runs — and your expiry moves out by that much. If your membership had already run out, the new period normally starts from the day you're renewed rather than from the expiry that had passed, so you still get the full term. They can grant bonus days on top of it, each with a reason, or set an exact expiry date by hand instead.

  • This period on your membership screen shows how the current period was built, right under the expiry it produced — for example "1 month + 7 bonus day(s)". It isn't shown when an admin set the expiry to an exact date, because a date on its own isn't built from a term and bonus days.
  • Booking a class spends a credit from the period you're in now — what you used up in an earlier one doesn't follow you into it.
  • A fixed pack starts counting from zero again, and the credits you hadn't used are normally carried over on top — so a renewed pack can show more than the number on its plan. An admin can switch that off for a renewal, and then the leftovers are forfeited.
  • Your hold allowance starts again from the day you were renewed.
  • Membership History, on the same screen, records every renewal, bonus grant and correction — with the reason and who made it.

If your membership ends

An expired membership doesn't remove you from the Space. You stay on its member list, your workout history is kept, and an admin can bring you back by adding a period — they can't send you a fresh invitation instead.

Don't want to look at a membership that has ended? Swipe its row in Account → My Memberships and tap Hide. That only takes the row off your own list — nothing is deleted, and it comes back on its own if the membership is renewed.

Membership holds

A hold pauses your membership for a range of dates — for a holiday, an injury, or any break. If your plan allows holds, you create them yourself from My Membership.

The rules

  • Your plan sets how many holds you may take and how many days in total. The allowance covers one membership period — the numbers above the list are headed This period — and either limit can be unlimited.
  • You can edit or cancel a hold until its start date arrives. Once it has started, only an admin or staff member can change it.
  • You can't book classes that fall inside a hold. Days either side of it are unaffected.
  • Holds can't overlap each other, be backdated, or fall outside your membership period.

If you already have bookings in that range

Those bookings are cancelled when the hold is created. Whether each credit comes back follows the same rules as cancelling yourself, so a hold can produce a mix of refunded and forfeited credits. The confirmation dialog spells out exactly what will happen before you commit.

What a renewal does to your holds

When an admin renews your membership, the allowance starts again from the day of the renewal. The holds you already took are kept, not deleted — they stay in the list. A hold that started before the day of the renewal stops counting against the new period's allowance; one that hasn't started yet still counts, because it falls inside the new period.

A hold that belongs to an earlier period carries an Earlier period caption and can't be edited or deleted — by you, or by an admin. Removing it now would pull your renewed expiry back by the days it had added, quietly taking away time you'd already been given.

Admins and staff can create, change or remove any member's hold in the current period, including ones that have already started. If you need a hold adjusted after it began, ask them — but an earlier period's hold is locked for them too.

Turn on booking for a Space

Booking is off by default and is turned on per Space. You need the Admin or Staff role, and it's available on every plan.

  1. Set the Space's time zone first. Every class time, day boundary and credit period is worked out in it, so changing it later shifts how existing schedules are read.
  2. Open Space Settings and turn on Enable Booking.
  3. Create at least one membership plan, then add your schedules.

On the free plan the limit you'll hit first is the 5-member cap, not booking itself — booking is not a paid-only feature.

Create and edit schedules

A schedule is one bookable class. From a Space with booking enabled, open the ⋯ menu and choose Add Schedule. Admins and staff can manage any schedule; coaches can be assigned to one but can't delete it.

What you set

  • Title and session — a name, and optionally the session it belongs to.
  • Date and time — start and end, read in the Space's time zone.
  • Repeat — never, daily or weekly, with an interval, chosen weekdays, and an end date.
  • Capacity — limited with a maximum, or unlimited.
  • Requires a credit — whether booking spends one of the member's credits.
  • Eligible memberships — restrict the class to certain plans. Leave it empty to open it to everyone. This works independently of whether a credit is required.
  • Coaches — who's running it. Their names show on the class.
  • Participant visibility — who can see the list of people booked. Defaults to managers only.
  • Booking opens and auto-cancel — how many days ahead booking opens, and whether the class cancels itself when the number booked is at or below a threshold, a set number of minutes before it starts.

Editing a repeating schedule

Changing one day of a repeating schedule asks what you meant, the way a calendar app does:

  • This Schedule Only — the change applies to that one day and the rest of the series is untouched.
  • Save for Future Schedules — the change applies from that day onwards. If you changed the repeat pattern itself, this is the only option offered, because the pattern belongs to the whole series.

Members who had booked are moved across automatically and notified, and their credits follow the class — including into a different week or month if the date moved.

Capacity can't be set below the number already booked for that day. Changing eligible memberships never cancels bookings that already exist — it applies from that point on.

Membership plans

A membership plan is the credit package you assign to a member. Plans work on every subscription tier and even without booking turned on — you can use them purely to track who's a member and until when.

What a plan defines

  • Name — what members see, like "Everyday" or "3 per week".
  • Duration — optional; how long one term of the plan runs, like 1 month. Inviting someone on a plan with a duration sets their expiry automatically to one term after their start date, and the field is read-only. After that it's the length Add period adds when you renew them — changing an existing member's plan in Manage Member doesn't move their expiry on its own.
  • Credits — per week, per month, a fixed pack, or unlimited, and how many.
  • Cancellation policy — always free, free up to a set number of hours before the class, or never free.
  • Holds — whether members may pause their membership, how many holds they get per period, and how many days in total.

Plan edits are not retroactive. A booking keeps the cancellation terms that were in force when it was made, so changing a policy today never changes what happens to a booking someone already holds.

Renewing a member

Renewals happen in Manage Member — open Community ▸ Members and tap the member's row. Everything on that screen is a draft: the bar at the bottom previews the expiry your save would write, and one Save commits the lot.

  • Add period — adds one or more terms of the member's plan and starts a new membership period. This is the renewal; there is no separate "Extend" button. If the member has already expired, the bar adds a Count from choice — From today (the default) or Continue from expiry → date — so you decide whether the lapsed days are given back or written off.
  • Bonus days — whole extra days on top, with a required reason (Promotion, Compensation, Referral, Loyalty, Other) and details that are optional unless the reason is Other. Up to 90 days in one save.
  • Set Expiry Date… — a correction, not a renewal. It asks before replacing a period you've added and any bonus days, and afterwards the member's This period line disappears, because an exact date isn't built from a term and bonus days.
  • Changing the plan or the start date never moves the expiry by itself — one of the three above has to do it. If you swap someone onto a new plan expecting a renewal, the screen says so and offers Add period.

Adding a period also restarts the member's hold allowance from that day, and starts a fresh credit period for a fixed pack. Membership History records every renewal, bonus grant and correction, with who made it.

Carrying credits over a renewal

When you renew someone on a fixed pack, Carry over remaining credits is on by default: the credits they hadn't used are added to the new period's allotment, and the row tells you how many. Switch it off for that renewal and those credits are forfeited instead — the row updates to say so before you save. It's a choice you make per renewal, not a plan setting.

You can't re-invite someone whose membership has expired. They're still on the roster — under the Expired tab in Members — so the invite screen refuses the send and points you at Manage Member → Add period, which is what reactivates them.

Participants, waitlist and holds

Open a schedule to see who's booked. Admins, staff and coaches can all view the roster; the actions below vary by role.

Booking and removing members

  • Add a member — you can override a full class (which books them over capacity), the credit charge, and the eligibility check. Adding someone also bypasses the booking window and the auto-cancel cutoff, so it works when self-booking is closed.
  • Remove a member — you choose whether to refund the credit. Removing frees a spot, so the first person on the waitlist is promoted automatically.

The waitlist

  • A Waitlist card lists everyone waiting, in order. It's visible to admins, staff and coaches only — members never see it, and there's no setting to change that.
  • You can add someone to the waitlist or remove them. Removing always refunds the held credit, because a waitlist hold never occupied a spot.

Holds and deletion

  • Admins and staff can create, edit or remove any member's hold in their current membership period, including one that has already started. Coaches can't.
  • A hold from an earlier period is shown but locked for everyone, admins included — changing it would drag the renewed expiry backwards and take away days the member was already given.
  • Deleting a schedule that has bookings asks for the scope — that one day or the whole series — and whether to refund the credits. Deleting is admin and staff only.

Increasing a full class's capacity doesn't promote anyone from the waitlist by itself. The next cancellation does. To fill the new spots straight away, add the members by hand.

Community posts

The Community tab is your Space's feed. Anyone who is a Member or above can post — share updates, photos, or a quick win.

  • A post can include text (up to 2,000 characters) and media — photos, videos, or YouTube links (up to 10, depending on your plan; free plans can attach 1).
  • Members can comment on and react to posts.
  • You can edit or delete your own posts at any time.
  • Staff and Admins can delete any post to moderate the feed, and can pin a post so it stays at the top (up to 5, on paid plans).

To post, open the Community tab and tap the new-post button.

PR Board

The PR Board is the Space's personal-record leaderboard. It lives in the Community tab as a card at the top of the feed and shows each member's best results for a set of preset movements — so everyone can see where they stand and chase the top of the board.

  • Preset movements — the board tracks preset exercises across 12 categories: Press, Deadlift, Squat, Snatch, Clean, Jerk, Olympic Lift, Gymnastic, Bodyweight, Endurance, Run, and Other. They're shown with a small waves icon (three curved bars, like a signal symbol) in the app.
  • Per Space — each Space you join has its own PR Board. Your records for preset movements are shared on the PR Board of every Space you've joined.
  • Shared vs. private — only your preset-movement records appear on the board. Custom exercises you add stay private in your own Personal Records (Account tab).

Add or update a record

  • From the PR Board, tap + to log a preset record. From Account → Personal Records you can also add custom exercises.
  • A record includes the exercise, a value with its unit, the measure type (weight, time, distance, or reps), and the date. For strength lifts you can log rep-maxes — 1RM, 3RM, 5RM, and so on, up to 20RM.
  • Tap any record to see its full history on a progress chart, with your best attempt marked. Filter the history by rep-max (1RM / 3RM / 5RM …).

Who can use it

Any Member of a Space can add records and appear on its PR Board — no special role or plan is required. Values display in your preferred units (kg/lb, km/mi); a record logged in another unit shows a converted badge. The board's detail view can also be filtered by category, gender, and age group.

Media Library

The Media Library lives in the Community tab. It's a shared store of reusable references — each item is one photo, video, or YouTube link with a title (up to 100 characters) and an optional note (up to 500 characters). Save a movement demo or briefing once, then attach it to as many workouts as you like instead of re-uploading.

  • Coaches, Staff, and Admins can add, edit, and archive items. Members can browse and use them.
  • Archive vs delete: archiving keeps the item and is reversible (managers can still see archived items; Members see only active ones). Deleting is permanent and removes it everywhere it's referenced.
Note: attaching media is plan-dependent (free plans can add 1 per workout or post, and the Media Library needs a paid plan), and a workout that uses Media Library items can't also have collaborating Spaces. See Create a Workout.

Invite members

From the Community tab, open Members and tap the invite (add-person) button. Invite someone by email or @username.

  • Coaches and above can invite new members.
  • New members join as Member by default. If your role allows it, you can pick a higher role when inviting.
  • Spaces are private — there's no public discovery. People join by invitation, or by scanning your Space's QR code and asking to join (see Join with a QR code).
  • Staff and Admins approve or decline each join request from the Members view.

Join with a QR code

Every Space has its own QR code. Anyone who scans it, in Showup or with their phone's camera app, can ask to join, and an Admin or Staff member approves each request — nobody gets in just by scanning.

Show your Space's QR code

From the Community tab, open Members and tap the QR code button next to the invite button. Coaches and above can open it.

  • Save to Photos — saves the code as an image, so you can print it and put it up in your Space.
  • Share QR Code — sends the image through your phone's share sheet.
  • Copy — copies the join link, for a message or an email. Tapping the link on a phone does the same as scanning the code.

A Space's code never changes, so a poster you've printed keeps working.

Scan a code to join

  1. On the Home tab, tap the + at the end of the My Spaces header and choose Join a space. If you don't have a Space yet, tap the Join a space tile on Home instead.
  2. The first time, Showup says why it needs the camera. Tap Continue and allow access. Nothing the camera sees is saved.
  3. Point the camera at the code. Once it's read, the frame turns green and the Space's page opens by itself.
  4. Tap Join, add a message if you'd like (up to 500 characters), and tap Send.

On the scanner:

  • Flashlight lights up the code in a dim room.
  • Load QR Code from Photo reads a code you were sent as an image, like a screenshot in a group chat.
  • A code that isn't a Showup Space's opens nothing — the scanner says "This isn't a Showup space QR code".

Camera access off? Tap Open Settings to turn it on, or load the code from a photo. Your phone's own camera app works too: point it at the code and tap the link that appears, and Showup opens on the same page. If you don't have the app yet, the link sends you to the App Store or Google Play — install it, sign in, then scan the code again.

Your request then waits under Requested on the Home tab. Tap it if you want to cancel. You're notified when it's accepted or declined, and once it's accepted the Space appears in My Spaces. If it's declined, or you cancel it, you can ask again.

Instead of Join, the page may show why there's nothing to send:

  • Pending — your request is waiting for an answer.
  • Invitation — you've already been invited. Tap it to answer the invitation.
  • Member — you're already in this Space.
  • Expired — your membership here has ended. Ask an Admin or Staff member of the Space to add a period.
  • Closed — the Space isn't taking join requests by QR right now.

Answer a join request

Admins and Staff are notified of each request. Open it from your notifications, or from Members → Pending → Join Requests. You'll see who is asking, when, and their message, with a link to their profile.

  • Accept Request adds them as a Member with no end date and no membership plan. To assign a membership plan, set an end date or change their role, tap them in the member list to open Manage Member.
  • Decline takes effect straight away — there's no confirmation.
  • An accepted request counts toward your plan's member limit. If the Space is full, you're asked to upgrade before you can accept.
  • Coaches can show and share the code, but they don't see or answer requests.

Pause QR joins

On the QR code page, Admins and Staff can turn off Accept join requests by QR. While it's off:

  • Every printed poster and shared link stops working — a single poster can't be retired on its own.
  • Requests already waiting can still be answered.
  • Current members, expired members and invitations you've already sent aren't affected.

You can turn it back on at any time, and the same code works again. Archiving a Space cancels the requests that were still waiting.

Manage members

Open Members from the Community tab to see everyone in the Space. Anyone in the Space can view and search the member list — unless an Admin or Staff member has turned on Hide member list from members in Space Settings. Then only Admins, Staff and Coaches can open it.

Staff and Admins can change a member's role and remove members, with a few limits:

  • You can't change your own role.
  • Staff can assign roles up to Staff. Nobody can be made an Admin: that role belongs to the person who created the Space.
  • Staff can't change or remove Admins.

Staff and Admins can also accept or decline join requests from the Members view. For the full breakdown of what each role can do, see Roles & permissions.

Edit your profile

Open the Account tab and tap your username at the top to edit your profile. You can change:

  • Username — lowercase and unique; availability is checked as you type. There's a cooldown, so it can only be changed once every so often.
  • Display name — required, up to 30 characters.
  • Date of birth, country, and gender.

Your email is shown here but isn't editable on this screen — see Email, password & sign out. Save becomes available once you've made a change and the username is valid. View My Profile opens your public profile exactly as other members see it.

Personal Records

Go to Account → Personal Records. This tracks your bests for preset exercises across 12 categories (Press, Deadlift, Squat, Snatch, Clean, Jerk, Olympic Lift, Gymnastic, Bodyweight, Endurance, Run, Other) as well as your own custom exercises. In the app, preset movements are marked with a small waves icon (three curved bars, like a signal symbol) so you can recognize them at a glance.

Add a record

  • Tap +, then choose a preset movement or create a custom exercise.
  • Enter the value with its unit, the measure type (weight, time, distance, or reps), and the date. For strength lifts you can log rep-maxes — 1RM, 3RM, 5RM, and so on, up to 20RM.

Edit or remove a record

Filter by category or search by name. Records for preset movements are shared on the PR Board of every Space you've joined; custom exercises stay private to you.

Benchmarks

Go to Account → Benchmarks. Benchmarks are standardized, named workouts with fixed movements (for example, Hero and Heroine WODs). They're different from Personal Records: a Personal Record tracks the best for a single exercise, while a benchmark tracks your best score for a whole, predefined workout.

  • Record / update — open a benchmark and enter your score or time. Only your best result per benchmark is kept, shown with its date.
  • Edit / remove — open the benchmark and edit or delete your recorded result.
  • Find one — search by name or movement (pull-up, pull_up, and pullup all match) or filter by category (Hero, Heroine, and so on).

Benchmarks are private to you and aren't shown on any PR Board. You can still choose whether they appear on your public profile — see Profile visibility.

If notifications are turned off at the system level, an Enable Notifications button appears and opens your device's permission prompt — on Android 13 and later that prompt also appears once, shortly after you first sign in. Categories stay disabled until both the system permission and the master switch are on.

Profile visibility & blocked users

Visibility

Account → Visibility controls what other people see on your public profile. Toggle each section on or off:

  • Showup Stats — your workout activity and streak data.
  • Personal Records — your exercise PRs and lifting history.
  • Benchmarks — your benchmark workout scores.
  • Communities — the gyms and groups you belong to.

Your own profile always shows every section; changes save immediately.

Blocked users

Account → Blocked Users lists everyone you've blocked, with the date. Swipe a row to unblock. People you block can't tell they're blocked, and their posts, comments, and notifications are hidden from you.

Subscription

Account → Subscription shows your current plan and billing cycle, the status (active, cancelled, or expired) with the renewal or expiry date, and the Spaces you own.

  • Change Plan — upgrade or downgrade your subscription.
  • Restore Purchases — re-sync your subscription with the store you bought it from, App Store or Google Play (e.g., on a new device).
  • Promo or offer codes can be redeemed where available.

Showup re-checks your subscription with the store when the app resumes and keeps your plan in sync automatically.

One subscription, all your Spaces

Your plan is attached to your account, not to a single Space. Every Space you create gets the full plan benefits independently — there is no separate charge per Space.

Where the money is handled

Prices are set and shown by the App Store and Google Play in your local currency; payment, renewal and receipts are handled by the store, not by us. Manage or cancel your subscription in the store you bought it from — and note that cancelling is a separate step from deleting your account.

Email, password & sign out

  • Update Email Address and Change Password appear only if you signed up with email (not Google or Apple). Changing your email sends a verification link to the new address; changing your password requires your current one.
  • Sign Out asks for confirmation before signing you out.
  • Contact Support opens a form that emails our support team. Privacy Policy and Terms of Service open in your browser.

Device limit

Your account can be signed in on a limited number of devices at once. If you try to sign in on one more than that, the sign-in is blocked and you'll see a screen explaining why.

  • You can use Showup on up to 2 devices at a time.
  • There's a 7-day cooldown between device changes, so you can't swap devices repeatedly.
  • To free a slot, sign out on a device you no longer use. If you're inside the cooldown, the screen shows how many days are left.

Signing out of a device you still have is the quickest fix. If you've lost the device or can't sign out on it, email support@showupcounts.com and we'll reset it for you.

Deactivate or delete your account

Deactivate

Deactivating is temporary and reversible: your account is hidden and you're signed out, but you can reactivate any time simply by signing back in. Note that this does not cancel your subscription — the store keeps billing until you cancel it (Google Play or App Store).

Delete

Deleting your Showup account is permanent and cannot be undone.

Before you delete, cancel any paid subscription. Billing is managed by the store, not by us, so deleting your account would not cancel it — which is why the Delete account button stays disabled while a paid subscription is still active. Cancel it first:

  • Android: Google Play Store → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Showup → Cancel.
  • iPhone / iPad: App Store → your Apple account → Subscriptions → Showup → Cancel.

How to delete (in the app):

  1. Open Showup and sign in.
  2. Go to the Account tab, then Account settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Type the name of each Space you own to confirm, then confirm the deletion.

If you can't access the app, email support@showupcounts.com from your account's email address with the subject "Delete my account", and we will verify ownership and process it.

What is deleted:

  • Your account and sign-in credentials (email/password and any Google, Apple, or Naver login).
  • Your profile, your username, and your app settings — including your registered devices and their push notification tokens.
  • Your fitness data: personal records, benchmark records, and workout scores.
  • Posts you wrote, together with their photos, videos, comments, and reactions.
  • Comments you wrote on other people's workouts, scores, and posts.
  • Your membership in every Space, and your notifications.

What is kept:

  • Spaces you created that still have other members are archived, not deleted, so those members keep their own score history and records.
  • Your booking and credit history in the Spaces you belonged to.
  • Support tickets you sent us, and content reports you filed.
  • Invitations you sent and join requests you made.
  • Your reactions on other people's content, and media or workouts you contributed to a Space you did not own — these stay part of that Space's records.
  • Membership history entries in a Space that name you.
  • Purchase and transaction records held by us, by our subscription provider, and by the app store, for the period required by tax, accounting, and consumer law.
  • A record that a deletion was requested and completed, kept as proof that we honoured your request.
  • Crash reports already collected.

If you want any of the retained items removed, email us and we will remove what we are not legally required to keep. Section 8 of the Privacy Policy sets all of this out in full.

Timing: deletion starts as soon as you confirm and is normally completed within minutes; in all cases it is fully completed within 30 days, except for data we are legally required to retain.
